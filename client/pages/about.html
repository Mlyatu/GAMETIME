Our Story

Built by players, for players

EFootball Arena started as a way to run fair campus tournaments without spreadsheets, group-chat chaos, and disputed scorelines. It grew into a full platform because that problem never goes away.

Our Mission

Every result should be trustworthy

Too many community tournaments fall apart over disputed scores, missed payouts, or an organizer who just stops responding. We built EFootball Arena so every match has a clear, auditable trail — from registration to the final whistle to the prize payout.

Fixtures generate themselves. Screenshots get read automatically. Standings update the second a result is approved. Nobody has to take anyone's word for it.
